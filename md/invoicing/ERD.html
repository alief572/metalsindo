PPN Calculation (DPP Nilai Lain)

Total_Harga = SUM(qty * harga_satuan) per item

DPP_Nilai_Lain = CEIL(11/12 * Total_Harga)
PPN            = DPP_Nilai_Lain * 12 / 100
Grand_Total    = Total_Harga + PPN

Sheet product: qty = SUM(stock_material.qty_sheet) WHERE no_kirim = id_do
Coil product:  qty = tr_invoice_detail.qty_invoice
